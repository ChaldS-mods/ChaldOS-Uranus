NVIDIA: Proprietary vs Open
NVIDIA (proprietary) — closed-source driver from NVIDIA. Maximum performance, best compatibility, works on all cards (GTX 900+). Choose this if unsure.
NVIDIA Open — open-source kernel module released by NVIDIA in 2022. Same performance on RTX 20/30/40 series. More transparent, easier to patch. Not recommended for older cards (GTX 900–1000 series).
